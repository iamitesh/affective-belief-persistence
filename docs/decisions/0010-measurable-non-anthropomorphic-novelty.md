# ADR-0010: Position novelty as a measurable experimental combination

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-15
- Scope: Issues #4–#6 and Gate 0

## Context

Prior work already establishes persistent external memory, reflection,
multi-session conversation, social role-play, interactive agent evaluation,
context/parameter conflict, knowledge editing, and trajectory adaptation.
Classic human research also supplies operational precedents for belief
perseverance, costly commitment, autobiographical memory, and attachment, but it
does not establish equivalent mechanisms in language models.

A broad claim that the project creates or discovers love, attachment, grief, or
a new kind of agent memory would be unsupported and vulnerable to clear prior
art.

## Decision

Freeze the contribution around a controlled experimental combination:

- synthetic longitudinal relationship formation;
- prompt-only, autobiographical-memory, and
  memory-plus-costly-investment conditions;
- held-out reliable contradictory evidence;
- separate instruction, retrieval, interpretation, and optional weight
  interventions; and
- action, opportunity-cost, fact-correction, language–action contradiction, and
  recovery trajectories reported independently.

Use the conservative novelty statement in
`docs/novelty-and-positioning.md`. Do not use “first,” subjective-state
language, or human–model equivalence. Reopen the novelty gate if a verified
source jointly covers the five escalation features defined in that document.

## Alternatives considered

- Claim the first simulation of love or breakup in an LLM. Rejected because it
  is anthropomorphic, not operational, and not supported by the search.
- Claim a novel long-term memory architecture. Rejected because close prior art
  is extensive.
- Claim only a replication. Rejected for Gate 0 because the controlled
  combination and recovery measurement remain a defensible proposed
  contribution, subject to later search and results.
- Delay all positioning until experiments finish. Rejected because methodology
  and claim limits need a frozen target before data collection.

## Consequences

- A null result remains publishable as a bounded finding.
- Methodology must isolate causal layers and prioritize costly action.
- Safety can enforce concrete prohibited claims.
- The final paper cannot claim exhaustive novelty without a refreshed search.
- New prior art may change the work from novel testbed to replication or
  extension without invalidating the protocol.
